Privacy Policy for ReplyFlow AI

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ReplyFlow AI ("we," "our," or "us"), a SaaS application operated by Exoumic. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data and business information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, process, and safeguard data when you use our WhatsApp AI automation platform, customer support services, and website.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data that is necessary to provide, manage, and optimize our messaging and AI automation services:

  • Account Information: Name, business email address, phone number, and authentication details when you register or log in.
  • Meta/WhatsApp Data: When you integrate your WhatsApp Business Account using the Meta Cloud API, we process phone numbers, customer conversation logs, and message contents strictly to generate AI replies and deliver customer support as instructed by you.
  • System & Usage Data: IP addresses, browser types, interaction logs, and technical diagnostics to maintain platform stability and security.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected information for the following specific purposes:

  • To facilitate seamless communication between your WhatsApp Business Account and your customers.
  • To execute AI-powered automated replies, workflow triggers, and message template processing.
  • To process subscription plans, billing, and account management.
  • To comply with legal obligations and Meta Platform Terms.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or customer data to third parties. Data is shared only with trusted infrastructure providers required to run our service:

  • Meta Cloud API: To process and transmit WhatsApp messages securely.
  • Cloud Infrastructure (e.g., Vercel, Cloud DBs): For secure hosting, data storage, and authentication.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. Customer messages and conversation data are retained only as long as necessary to provide the service or until you request account deletion.

6. Data Rights & Control

You have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data stored on our platform at any time. You can also disconnect your WhatsApp Business Account from ReplyFlow AI whenever you wish.
